Feather of Forever: Part 2

You bow slightly to the feather (for some reason?) and back out.
As you do, a hidden panel slides open. Inside is a glowing stone, engraved with “43.”
You pick up the stone.
As you grip it, your vision blurs and the library begins to spin. When everything dies down, you are on the top of an obsidian staircase, leading deeper underground.
The way behind is blocked. There is nothing there, only a cave wall. A new chapter begins…
