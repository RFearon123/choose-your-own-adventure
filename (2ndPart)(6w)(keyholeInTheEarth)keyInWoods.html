The Keyhole in the Earth: Part 2

You knock politely.
A moment later, a tiny stone golem pops out of the ground and says, “Finally, someone with manners.”
He hands you a scroll titled “How Not To Die Underground – Beginner Edition.”
You’ve earned the golem’s trust. Knowledge is power.
With a nod of approval, the golem vanishes into the earth, leaving behind a faint trail of glowing dust—and the sense that something much deeper has just begun.
As you unroll the scroll, a gust of wind whirls around you—and in the blink of an eye, you find yourself back home, the dirt of the underground still clinging to your boots.
