The Key in the Woods

Chapter 2

I picked it up. It was heavier than I expected, cold to the touch, and had the number 42 engraved on it.
That’s when I noticed a path I’d never seen before, narrow and winding, leading deeper into the woods. The air felt still. Curious, I stepped onto it.
After about ten minutes of walking, I found something unbelievable: a small wooden door, set into the side of a mossy hill. There was a keyhole.
