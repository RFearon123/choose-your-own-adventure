The Key in the Woods

Chapter 4

You list it as “Mystery Key – Possibly Cursed – Number 42.”
It sells for $1,337 to a user named “gnome_lover_666.”
You never hear from them again.
Three weeks later, your mailbox contains… another key.
With the message…

"You cannot escape the key.
It is your destiny.

You don't want it.
You didn't want it.
But, it seems you can't get rid of it.
It follows you- even when you forget on purpose.
When you moved house and left it behind.
It always appears again with a new reminder, in your pocket.
You tried to inform the police, several times. But, they couldn't see the key or the message. In fact, they would often forget the conversation as soon as you mentioned the key or showed to them. You stopped trying to inform the authorities because it was obviously, a problem they couldn't help you with. It made feel like you were going insane. Twenty years later, it was returned to you again. This time with an addendum to the previously, always consistent message.
They had clearly gotten fed up. Whoever "they" were anyway.

"You cannot escape the key.
It is your destiny.
We won't tell you again.
Next time, you leave it. You won't live to regret the consequences.

You don't dare try again. Not with the threatening message.
So you keep it.

You never use it. Not once in your entire life. You don't seek out the mystery surrounding it.
Not once, not ever.
You live life but always with a touch of wariness, a hint of caution, always looking over your shoulder, always watching and wondering and waiting.

It's exhausting.

Eventually, you grow older, frailer, and start to lose your memory. You forget why the key is so significant and you die.
The key vanishes from your house.
Your descendants search for it. Assuming, it was important to you as you often kept it close. But they never find it.

It has gone back home, to the forest, you visited in your youth.

Waiting for its next victim…

May they be luckier than you…

ENDING
