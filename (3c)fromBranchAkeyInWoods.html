The Key in the Woods

Chapter 3

I turned the key. It clicked—loudly. The door creaked open, revealing stairs that spiraled down into darkness.
There was a flashlight on the wall. Oddly convenient. I grabbed it and descended.
The staircase ended in a stone chamber filled with… rubber ducks. Hundreds of them. One of them had a note taped to its belly:
“CHOOSE WISELY.”
In the middle of the room were three levers.
Which lever do you pull?

G. The lever shaped like a duck.

H. The lever glowing faintly green.

I. The rusty lever labeled “DO NOT TOUCH.”
