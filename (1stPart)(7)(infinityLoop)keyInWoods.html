The Key in the Woods

The Infinity Loop: Part 1 (The final dumb adventure zone)

You step into the swirling portal. Suddenly you’re on a floating island, surrounded by clones of… yourself?

A glowing sign hovers in the air: “CHOOSE THE RIGHT YOU.”

Each clone holds an object:

  • One holds a duck.
  • One holds a pizza.
  • One holds a mirror.
  • One is holding... two keys and doing the worm dance.

Do you …

ZE. Pick the duck-you. Ducks have been a theme.

ZF. Pick the pizza-you. Maybe this is a snack puzzle.

ZG. Pick the mirror-you. Gotta reflect, right?

ZH. Pick the worm-dancing key-you. Obviously chaos is the answer.
