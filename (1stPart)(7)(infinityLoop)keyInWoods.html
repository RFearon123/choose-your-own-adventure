The Key in the Woods

The Infinity Loop: Part 1

You step into the swirling portal. Suddenly you’re on a floating island, surrounded by clones of… yourself?

A glowing sign hovers in the air:

“CHOOSE THE RIGHT YOU.”

Each clone holds an object:

One holds a rubber duck.
One holds a pizza.
One holds a mirror.
One is holding... two keys and doing the worm dance.

Do you …

Pick the duck-you. Who doesn't love a rubber duck?

Pick the pizza-you. Maybe this is a snack puzzle.

Pick the mirror-you. Gotta reflect, right?

Pick the worm-dancing key-you. Obviously chaos is the answer.
