The Key in the Woods

The Door of Ending Things: Part 1

You yell something dramatic like, “I REJECT YOUR NARRATIVE!”

And then dropkick the door.

It explodes into pixels.

The world flickers and glitches. The spirit-frogs gasp. Leafy Larry screams “NOOOO THAT’S NOT IN THE SCRIPT—!”

Suddenly you're in a new dimension, holding a paintbrush and a keyboard. A raccoon in a hoodie hands you a mug that says “CEO of Weirdness.”

You realize:

You now control the story.
You can write anything.
The key… was imagination all along.
