The Key in the Woods

The Door of Ending Things: Part 1

You now stand before the final door. It hums with an energy older than time itself: woven from stardust, twisted bark, and fragments of forgotten memes that flicker across its surface like forgotten relics of the internet’s past. A Shrek gif glitches beside a galaxy swirl. A faint voice echoes:
“Everything is as it should be.”

The wood pulses with life.
The stars wink knowingly.
The memes judge you silently.

Above it, glowing letters shift like constellations rearranging themselves just for you:

“To leave the Loop, you must choose how your story ends.”

The door has no handle- only the feeling that it's waiting. Watching.
It knows the paths you’ve taken.
The keys you’ve carried.
The secrets you've buried.
Around you, echoes of every choice you’ve made reverberate like whispers through the trees.

Behind it, something final stirs. A truth. A goodbye. Or… something else entirely?

The loop is almost closed.

And it’s up to you to decide what that means…

You see three glowing paths:

Escape and forget everything and resume your ordinary existence.

Embrace the Keymaster’s fate and become the new guardian.

Shatter the door and make your own ending.
