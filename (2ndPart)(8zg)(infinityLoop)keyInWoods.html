The Key in the Woods

The Infinity Loop: Part 2

You gaze into the mirror. It shimmers, warps, and then reveals… not your usual face, but a very confused raccoon wearing your hoodie. Paws clutch the fabric like it’s borrowed. Eyes wide. A little snack in one hand.

“The truth hurts,” the mirror whispers, as the raccoon-you blinks back at you, caught between dumpster instincts and deep existential dread.

You take a step back, but the reflection doesn't—raccoon-you just stares harder, like it's trying to figure out taxes or the meaning of dreams.

And yet… something clicks. You suddenly understand things—like how moonlight tastes, and why cereal hits different at 3 a.m.

Your third eye opens. Literally. And then, with a soft pop, you vanish from the room.

YOU GAIN: Self-knowledge +2 wisdom.
STATUS: Teleported to The Door of Ending Things.
