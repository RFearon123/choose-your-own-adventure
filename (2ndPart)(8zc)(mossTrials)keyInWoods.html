The Key in the Woods

The Moss Trials: Part 2

You decide to yeet the goo. You're definitely not chancing that mushroom and hopefully, skin contact with goo will be less dangerous than ingesting glowing mushrooms.

You scoop up the goo with great effort—it's thick, cold, and stretches like warm mozzarella, clinging to your hands with a stubborn squelch. It slides around too quickly to aim properly, so you decide to throw it as fast as you can before it escapes your grip entirely, launching it in a wild arc that splatters onto…

…

…onto a judge spirit.

The spirit screams and then dissolves into a puddle.
“YOU YEETED THE ELDER.”
Alarms go off. Vines grab your ankles. You are turned into a decorative bonsai.

Cause of death: Goocide.
