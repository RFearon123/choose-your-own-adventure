The Key in the Woods

CThe Moss Trials: Part 1

"Welcoming contestant number 42…" (You are now contestant 42 in a secret forest spirit game show)

You’re standing on a velvet stage, under neon mushrooms that pulse like disco lights. A vine microphone descends from the ceiling.
A floating spirit host in a sequined cloak shouts:
“WELCOME to The Moss Trials! I’m your host, Leafy Larry, and tonight you’ll face three challenges to prove you’re worthy of the Keymaster’s path!”
A crowd of ghostly animals cheers.

Your first challenge is projected on a giant fern:
“TRIAL ONE: EAT OR YEET”

A table appears. On it:
A glowing mushroom labeled “Yes.”
A bowl of jiggly green goo labeled “Nope.”
A squirrel holding a spoon and watching you intently.
Do you:

Eat the mushroom. YOLO.

Yeet the goo. Hard.

Feed the squirrel and hope for mercy.
