The Key in the Woods

Chapter 3

If you chose F (Forget about it):
You move on. Life goes on. You grow up, get a job, maybe a cat.
But some nights, when the wind is just right, you swear you hear a faint whisper:
“...forty-two…”
One day, a package arrives in the mail. No return address. Inside is a rubber duck… with a key inside it. Do you:

P. Try the key on random doors around town.

Q. Smash the duck and bury the key.

R. Sell the key on eBay for £4.99 and forget about it.
