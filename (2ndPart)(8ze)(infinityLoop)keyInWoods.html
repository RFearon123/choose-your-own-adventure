The Key in the Woods

The Infinity Loop: Part 2

You high-five Duck-You. It honks—loudly, triumphantly. For a moment, everything feels victorious.

Then, the sky darkens. A shadow blots out the sun. You both look up just in time to see an enormous celestial duck descending from the heavens, beak wide open like the gates of doom

Without time to scream, you're both swallowed whole in one tremendous QUACK.
Inside is warm, wet, and suspiciously bread-scented. The last thing you hear is Duck-You whispering, “Worth it.”

Cause of death: Cosmic quack-backlash.
