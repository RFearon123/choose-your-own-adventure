The Key in the Woods

Chapter 4

You turn to see a tall figure cloaked in leaves. It hands you a glowing stone and a key
The figure whispers, “You still have time.”
Then it disappears.
You look down—the stone has the number 43 on it and the key is engraved with the number 42.
You relise the key is, the key that you left in the woods. It's thing that started this whole adventure, in the first place.
You’ve been chosen. For what? You don’t know. Yet…
You hold the glowing stone as the forest shifts. Trees part, revealing an obsidian staircase leading underground.
