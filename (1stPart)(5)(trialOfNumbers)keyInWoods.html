The Key in the Woods

The Trial of Numbers: Part 1

Torches light as you descend.

At the bottom is a round chamber. On the wall: a door with two keyholes—one labeled 42, the other 43.

As you grip the stone marked '43,' a soft warmth spreads through your hand—then, as if summoned by the magic itself, a key etched with '43' shimmers into existence inside your pocket.

From your pocket, the 43 key hums.

A voice booms:
“Two keys. Two choices. Only one opens the path.”

Do you:

Insert both keys at the same time.

Try just the 42 key.

Yell “I FORFEIT!” and run.
